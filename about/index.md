<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p>Builds embedded systems that behave predictably in the real world, from bare-metal firmware to the desktop tools, test rigs and build infrastructure that support them. Currently at LMesh IoT, on medical and industrial devices with Zephyr, FreeRTOS and BLE/Wi-Fi stacks.</p>
<p>Prefers explicit state machines over clever control flow, reproducible builds, and code the next engineer can continue without a s&eacute;ance.</p>

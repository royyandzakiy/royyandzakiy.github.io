Quieter terminal chrome

Six options for toning down the "terminal" parts (the $ commands, the -rw-r--r-- royyan 1.5K columns, the front-matter block, prev/next) so the post list and the post itself stand out. Each is drawn at real size (1280 px wide) and scaled down. A is today's site.

A. Current

For reference: commands in full black with a green $, permissions in mid grey, dates orange, titles blue.

B. Quiet chrome

Same layout; commands, the $, permission columns and front matter fade to light grey. Titles keep their blue, dates go a softer orange. The smallest change.

C. Ghost

Commands at very low contrast. The list shows only date + title (no permissions, owner, size). The front-matter block becomes one quiet line: date · tags. The most content-first.

D. Monochrome

No green or orange anywhere. Titles in dark bold with no underline until hover; everything else grey. Calm and very readable; less "terminal colour", same terminal shape.

E. Paper card

The list and the post sit on a white card with a border, while the commands and footer stay on the grey page. Makes "this is the content" vs "this is the frame" obvious at a glance.

F. Small chrome

Commands, permission columns and front matter in smaller grey type; content stays full size. A dashed line separates the post from prev/next.
